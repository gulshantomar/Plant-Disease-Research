6. MODEL COMPARISON: MobileNetV2 vs EfficientNetB0
   
   Performance:
   - MobileNetV2:    Accuracy 85.11%, F1 85.14%
   - EfficientNetB0: Accuracy 87.05%, F1 87.08%
   - Difference:     +1.94% (EfficientNetB0 higher)
   
   Deployment Characteristics:
   
   Model             Size    Parameters  Speed        Deployment
   MobileNetV2       9.2MB   2.25M       45ms         Mobile/Edge
   EfficientNetB0    24.5MB  5.1M        78ms         Cloud/Server
   
   → Interpretation: "EfficientNetB0 achieves approximately 2% higher accuracy 
     at the cost of 2.7x larger model size and 1.7x inference time. This trade-off 
     is particularly relevant for deployment scenarios: mobile applications favor 
     MobileNetV2's efficiency, while server-based systems can leverage 
     EfficientNetB0's marginal accuracy advantage. The comparable F1-scores suggest 
     both models are viable for production deployment."
